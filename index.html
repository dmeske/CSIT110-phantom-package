Phantom Package

An AI coding assistant tells you to install a package. Some suggestions are real and safe. Some don't exist, some are brand-new copies of names AI tools invent (slopsquatting), and some are look-alikes of popular packages. Look things up, then decide.

Each round is worth 4 points. Every lookup costs 1 point (a correct call always earns at least 1). Installing a bad package costs 2 points. Rejecting a safe one earns 0. 8 rounds, 32 points possible. Every package and registry detail here is invented for practice. No timer.

Keyboard: when focus isn't on a button, press 1–4 for lookups, I to install, or D for don't install.
